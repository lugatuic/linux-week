					\pause
				\item Did NOT use UNIX
					\pause
				\item MIT Hacker Culture
					\pause
				\item Connected to ARPANET
					\pause

					funding
					\pause
				\item UNIX had corporate support/money on top

					more than the necessity it was for

					it became almost a religion
